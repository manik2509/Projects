Technical Architecture & Engineering

Solid-Fuel Chemistry & Thermodynamics

Utilized chemical engineering principles to optimize the solid propellant grain geometry and combustion dynamics. Evaluated burn rates, specific impulse (Isp), and chamber pressure to ensure a stable and consistent thrust curve.

Nozzle Design & CAD Integration

Designed the converging-diverging (De Laval) nozzle utilizing CAD software. Optimized the expansion ratio to maximize exhaust gas velocity at ambient atmospheric pressure, ensuring peak aerodynamic efficiency.

Data Analysis & Testing Protocol

  • Static Test Fires: Orchestrated multiple live static test fires of the rocket motor. Implemented strict safety compliance and risk mitigation protocols prior to ignition.
  • Data Acquisition: Captured real-time thrust and pressure data using load cells and DAQ systems.
  • MATLAB Optimization: Processed the raw test data using MATLAB and Origin to plot thrust curves, analyze anomalies, and calibrate the theoretical models against empirical data.
